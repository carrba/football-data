# Metric Definitions
- **Progressive Runs**: Ball carries that move possession meaningfully toward the opponent's goal, using location-based distance thresholds. A run is typically counted as progressive when it advances the ball by at least:
    - **30 meters** if the carry starts and ends in the player's **own half**
    - **15 meters** if the carry starts in the **own half** and ends in the **opponent's half**
    - **10 meters** if the carry starts and ends in the **opponent's half**
- **Pressures**: Defensive actions where a player actively applies immediate pressure to the on-ball opponent, aiming to disrupt or force a decision. A pressure is typically counted when the defender:
    - closes to a contesting distance quickly (not just occupying nearby space)
    - clearly orients body/run to challenge the ball carrier or likely receiver
    - triggers a rushed action (pass, touch, clearance, or shot) or a visible change in direction
    - does so while the opponent has control or is about to receive the ball
  Actions are usually **not** counted as pressures when a player only screens space, holds shape without engaging, or marks loosely without affecting the opponent's action.
- **Recoveries**: Instances where a player regains control of a loose or second ball for their team.
- **Clearances**: Defensive actions that intentionally remove the ball from danger, usually away from the defending goal.

- **Chances Created**: Count of shot-ending passes by a player.
- **Big Chances Created**: Count of shot-ending passes by a player where the resulting shot is explicitly marked as a **Big Chance**. A **Big Chance** is a chance where the shot taker is expected to score, e.g. 1v1 with the keeper with no nearby defenders.
- **Touches in Opposition Box**: Number of times a player takes a controlled touch while in the opponent's penalty area.

- **Interceptions**: Defensive actions where a player reads and cuts out an opponent's pass by moving into the passing lane and gaining control, or clearly redirecting the ball to a teammate. Touches that only deflect the ball without meaningful team retention are usually not counted as interceptions.
- **Aerial Duels (Won/Lost)**: Contested ball actions in the air where an **opponent is actively challenging for the same ball**. If there is no direct challenge, it is not logged as a duel.
    - **Won**: The player reaches/controls the ball first
    - **Lost**: The opponent wins the contest.

- **Ground Duels (Won/Lost)**: 1v1 contested ball actions on the ground where an **opponent is actively attempting to dispossess**. Non-contested touches are not duels.
    - **Won**: The player dispossesses the opposition player in possession by challenging for the ball. The team that retains possession after the contested duel is not material. Loose balls, i.e. 50/50 challenges are also classed as duels.
    - **Lost**: The opponent attempts to dispossess a player in possession but fails. The opponent losses the duel and the player in possession wins the duel.
- **Tackles**: The defender makes a legal, ground‑level challenge and takes the ball away from an opponent in controlled possession.

- **Pack Passes**: A pass that eliminates one or more opposition players by moving the ball past them, leaving them behind the ball and no longer able to participate in the defensive phase.
- **Pack Pass Score** A score is calculated for a pass depending on the player's position that was bypassed. A pass taking out a single defender is 3 points, a single midfielder is 2 points and a single attacker is 1 point, with the total being the sum. E.g. "Packing" two defenders and a midfielder would gain a pack pass score of 8 points.
- **Pack Receipts**: The receiver of a pack pass. The receiver gets a pack receipt score equivilant to the pack passer's pass score.
- **Pack Dribble**: Player in possession gets beyond an opposition player while in control of the ball. Score is attributed in the same points system i.e. a defender is 3 points, a midfielder is 2 points and an attacker is 1 point.
- **Pack Turnovers**: Turnovers (lost possession) that occur during a pass or dribble that results in lost possession with teammates ahead of the ball and effectively taken out of the current phase of play. Scoring is the same as for passes, i.e. a defender is 3 points, a midfielder is 2 points and an attacker is 1 point. Again, it is calculated as the sum of the players bypassed.
