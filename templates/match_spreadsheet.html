Column Legend

Short C: Short Completed Passes
Short I: Short Incomplete Passes
Short %: Short Pass Completion %
Long C: Long Completed Passes
Long I: Long Incomplete Passes
Long %: Long Pass Completion %
Pack P: Pack Passes
Pack Score: Pack Pass Score
Pack Recv: Pack Pass Receive Score
Overall %: Overall Pass Completion % (Pack + Short + Long) / (Pack + Short + Long + Incomplete)
Pack Turn: Pack Turnovers
PT Score: Pack Turnover Score
xA: Sum of xG values from shots where this player is the assister
Ch Cr: Chances Created (shot-ending passes)
Big Ch Cr: Big Chances Created (shot-ending passes to big chances)
Assists: Assisted passes that resulted in goals
xG: Total expected goals from all shots taken by the player in the match
Goals: Goals scored by the player
A Won: Aerial Duels Won
A Lost: Aerial Duels Lost
G Won: Ground Duels Won
G Lost: Ground Duels Lost
Tackles W: Tackles Won
Int: Interceptions
Clear: Clearances
Press: Pressures
Fouls C: Fouls Committed
Fouls W: Fouls Won
Offs: Offsides
Prog R: Progressive Runs
Opp Box Tch: Touches in Opposition Box
